About me

I’m Abhinav Shaw. I combine AI and systems engineering to solve problems in the physical world, with a particular interest in robotics, autonomous driving, and embodied AI.

My early work at the University of Massachusetts Amherst, where I studied computer science, focused on machine learning for health. I explored how models could learn from mobile sensor data to understand and predict individual stress levels, contributing to research on personalized and multitask learning.

From there, I moved into autonomous driving at Lyft and later Woven by Toyota, where I work today. That journey brought my interests in machine learning and large-scale software together around systems that interact with the real world.

I’m deeply passionate about learning. Alongside a full-time job, I continued my education at Stanford, deepening my understanding of AI and exploring questions in cooperative multi-agent learning.

A strong foundation in computer science and a systems-thinking approach shape how I work. I look beyond an individual model to understand how algorithms, data, software, and real-world constraints fit together. I’m especially drawn to embodied AI: building intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and act in the physical world.

Outside of work, I enjoy playing guitar, football, and Dota 2. You can find my code on GitHub or connect with me on LinkedIn.

Cooperative Agents through Indirect Communication

A Stanford project exploring stigmergy: agents sharing information through changes to their environment rather than direct messages. Compared communication-enabled and baseline agents across team sizes and critic architectures, observing modest, consistent improvements in success rate and return.
